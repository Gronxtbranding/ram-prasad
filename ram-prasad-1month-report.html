01 — Executive Summary

Month 1 Overview

Month 1 establishes CA Ram Prasad’s presence as a senior Finance Transformation and CFO-level leader on LinkedIn. 3,189 total impressions were generated across 12 posts, with Post 7 delivering the highest reach at 456 impressions and Post 11 achieving the highest engagement rate at 7.36% — driven by 11 likes and 8 comments. A targeted outreach of 238 connection requests to senior leaders at Deloitte, SAP, Accenture, Oracle, Adobe, IBM, Unilever, and others yielded 88 accepted connections (~37%). The month generated 5 inbound opportunities spanning senior Finance roles in Dubai, a Group Business Controller position, a Treasury Leader role, and an F&A Transformation mandate at Wipro.

05 — Inbound Opportunities

Opportunities Received

5 inbound opportunities were received in Month 1 — a strong signal of early visibility and positioning. These include international Finance roles in Dubai, a Group Business Controller position, an F&A Transformation mandate at Wipro, and strategic professional networking from a senior supply chain consulting leader.

Senior Finance Role in Dubai
Simran Patel · Global Executive Search Specialist, CXO & Senior Leadership Hiring

Simran reached out recruiting for a senior Finance role for a client in Dubai. She asked CA Ram Prasad to share his direct contact number and an updated CV to discuss the opportunity further.

Group Business Controller – RGE Group
Kelly Lim · Talent Acquisition, RGE Group

Kelly reached out regarding a Group Business Controller opportunity at RGE Group — a privately owned conglomerate with core expertise in fibre, palm oil, and oil & gas.

Treasury Leader – DAMAC Group, Dubai
Zein Hawari · Group Talent Acquisition Director, DAMAC Group

Zein shared an opening for a Treasury Leader in Dubai, focused on raising funds for DAMAC Group’s global data centre business. Offered to discuss the role over a Teams call.

Senior Manager – F&A Transformation, Wipro
Kumud Singh · Deputy Manager, Talent Acquisition, Wipro

Kumud shared a Senior Manager F&A Transformation opportunity based in Chennai. The role is hybrid, requires working UK-shift hours (12 PM to 9:30 PM IST), and 13–18 years of relevant Finance Transformation experience.

Professional Networking – NexWave Consulting
Ranga Davala, SCOR-P, CSCP, F.I.I.E · Founder, NexWave SAP Consulting

Ranga accepted the connection and reached out to exchange perspectives on supply chain and finance. He shared his contact number for further dialogue.
